About Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. Human activities such as burning fossil fuels contribute significantly to global warming.

Climate Facts

  • 🌡️ Global temperatures are rising each year.
  • 🌊 Sea levels are increasing due to melting ice caps.
  • 🌳 Trees absorb carbon dioxide and help combat climate change.
  • ♻️ Recycling reduces waste and saves resources.
